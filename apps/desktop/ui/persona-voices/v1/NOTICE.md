# Bundled persona dialogue voices

The 544 Ogg Opus files in this directory were generated locally with MediaTek Research's BreezyVoice-300M model. The model and inference code are available under Apache-2.0.

Before they were hash-listed, the clips were levelled in the voice lab: one constant gain each towards EBU R128 -23 LUFS under a -1 dBTP true-peak ceiling, and a 3 ms fade at each edge. Those two figures are what the gain aimed at, not a measurement of the files in this directory: a clip whose peaks would have crossed the ceiling was given less gain than the target asked for, and encoding to Opus moves both numbers again. Measured on these Ogg files the integrated loudness runs from -24.9 to -22.8 LUFS and the loudest true peak is -0.97 dBTP. Some clips were also trimmed at one end: a tail where speech recognition heard words the written line does not contain, or a head or a tail where the clip opened or closed on a stretch of room tone more than 35 dB below its own loudest moment. Each trim leaves about 120 ms of natural lead-in or run-out, and every trimmed clip was re-transcribed to confirm the cut had taken none of the written line with it: no character the same transcriber heard before the cut could be missing after it. That is a before-and-after comparison; these transcribers also mishear characters on clips that were never trimmed, so it is not a claim that any transcript matches the line exactly. Clips that trimming could not fix were re-recorded instead: two speech recognition models trained on different data both transcribed syllables the written line does not contain, and the silence trim above had already run on those files without removing them. Each was re-synthesised until the two models no longer agreed on any syllable beyond the written line, and the new take had to pass the same loudness, true-peak and speaker-identity checks as the one it replaced. No compression, limiting, or other dynamics processing was applied.

This directory holds only the Ogg files, `manifest.json` with its `manifest.js` mirror, and this notice. The WAV masters, the reference recordings the voices were conditioned on, and the quality-control receipts stay in the voice lab and are not published.

The generated persona voice files are excluded from this repository's Apache-2.0 license.
