# Bundled persona banter voices

The 340 Ogg Opus files in this directory were generated locally with MediaTek Research's BreezyVoice-300M model. The model and inference code are available under Apache-2.0.

Before they were hash-listed, the clips were levelled in the voice lab: one constant gain each to EBU R128 -23 LUFS under a -1 dBTP true-peak ceiling and a 3 ms fade at each edge. Some clips were also trimmed at one end: a tail where speech recognition heard words the written line does not contain, or a head or a tail where the clip opened or closed on a stretch of room tone more than 35 dB below its own loudest moment. Each trim leaves about 120 ms of natural lead-in or run-out, and every trimmed clip was re-transcribed to confirm that no character of the written line went missing. Clips that trimming could not fix were re-recorded instead: two speech recognition models trained on different data both transcribed syllables the written line does not contain, and the silence trim above had already run on those files without removing them. Each was re-synthesised until the two models no longer agreed on any syllable beyond the written line, and the new take had to pass the same loudness, true-peak and speaker-identity checks as the one it replaced. No compression, limiting, or other dynamics processing was applied.

This directory holds only the Ogg files, `manifest.json` with its `manifest.js` mirror, and this notice. The WAV masters, the reference recordings the voices were conditioned on, and the quality-control receipts stay in the voice lab and are not published.

The generated persona voice files are excluded from this repository's Apache-2.0 license. Ted Huang granted AI-Sister permission on 2026-09-12 to include the exact, unmodified files listed by `manifest.json` in the source tree and official builds. This grant does not extend to any source recording, private receipt, training corpus, or voice-lab working file.
