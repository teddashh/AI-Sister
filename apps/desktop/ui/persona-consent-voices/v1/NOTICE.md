# Bundled persona consent-reading voices

The 68 Ogg Opus files in this directory were generated locally with MediaTek Research's BreezyVoice-300M model. The model and inference code are available under Apache-2.0.

Before they were hash-listed, the clips were levelled in the voice lab: one constant gain each to EBU R128 -23 LUFS under a -1 dBTP true-peak ceiling and a 3 ms fade at each edge. Some clips were also trimmed at one end: a tail where speech recognition heard words the written line does not contain, or a head or a tail where the clip opened or closed on a stretch of room tone more than 35 dB below its own loudest moment. Each trim leaves about 120 ms of natural lead-in or run-out, and every trimmed clip was re-transcribed to confirm that no character of the written line went missing. A few clips were re-recorded instead of edited: two speech recognition models trained on different data both transcribed syllables the written line does not contain, and those syllables run straight into the first word, leaving nothing to trim. Each was re-synthesised until neither model heard anything beyond the line, and the new take had to pass the same loudness, true-peak and speaker-identity checks as the one it replaced. No compression, limiting, or other dynamics processing was applied.

The generated voice files are excluded from this repository's Apache-2.0 license. This grant does not extend to source recordings, private receipts, training corpora, WAV files, QC reports, or voice-lab work.
